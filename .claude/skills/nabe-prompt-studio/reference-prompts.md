# Canonical NABE prompts (gold standard)

These two prompts define the target quality and grammar. Every NABE prompt
should feel like a sibling of these.

## 1. Mature woman — die-cut sticker

> A die-cut sticker design of a smiling mature woman with shoulder-length
> straight brown hair, drawn strictly in a hyper-stylized retro comic aesthetic
> resembling classic Argentine comic strips. She has exaggerated cute
> proportions, a large round face, a prominent nose, and simplified dot-like
> eyes. Watercolor illustration style, expressive thin black ink outlines,
> delicate watercolor washes. She is wearing a simple red sweater. Completely
> solid pure white background, thick white contour border, strictly isolated
> subject with clean edges for sticker production.

## 2. Young girl crocheting — isolated illustration

> A stylized young girl with voluminous messy black hair featuring a dark
> watercolor wash texture, and a prominent red hair bow. She has a round face
> and is smiling happily. She is sitting down, highly focused on crocheting a
> small, cute amigurumi bear toy using a single crochet hook and yarn. She is
> wearing her iconic vibrant red trapeze dress with a white rounded Peter Pan
> collar, white socks, and black shoes. Watercolor illustration style,
> expressive thin black ink outlines, delicate artistic washes. Completely
> solid pure white background, no drop shadows, strictly isolated subject.

## Why they work
- Specific subject read (age, hair texture, expression).
- A concrete action or prop (crocheting an amigurumi bear).
- The full NABE style spine is present.
- Explicit production finishing (white background, contour, isolated).
